About the Professional Tour Guide

Tsegay Measho Baraki – Professional Tour Guide

My name is Tsegay Measho Baraki. I am from Axum, Ethiopia, one of the most historically important cities in Africa.

I have been working as a local tour guide since 2005 E.C. and hold a professional tour guide license. I am passionate about sharing the history, culture, traditions and heritage of Ethiopia with visitors from around the world.

I offer guided experiences in Axum and other remarkable destinations across Ethiopia. My goal is to help visitors discover the country's historical treasures and cultural diversity.

Languages: Tigrinya, Amharic and English.

Based in: Axum, Ethiopia.
