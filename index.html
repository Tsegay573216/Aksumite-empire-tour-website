About the Professional Tour Guide

Tsegay Measho Baraki

I am a professional local tour guide from Axum, Ethiopia. I have been working as a professional tour guide since 2005 E.C. (2012/2013 G.C.).

I am a licensed professional tour guide through the Culture and Tourism Bureau of Tigray Region.

I speak Tigrinya, Amharic and English.

Based in Axum, I offer guided tours and travel experiences in Axum and different destinations across Ethiopia.

I personally assist visitors with their travel arrangements, airport pickups, hotel transfers, historical visits and customized tour experiences.

My goal is to help visitors discover the history, culture, traditions and natural beauty of Ethiopia.

Discover Axum

The Great Stelae

Discover the remarkable ancient stone obelisks that tell the story of the Aksumite civilization.

St. Mary of Zion

Visit one of Ethiopia's most important historic religious sites, deeply connected with Ethiopian Orthodox Christian traditions.

Ancient Royal Tombs

Explore the royal tombs and archaeological treasures connected with the ancient Aksumite kings.

King Ezana's Inscription

Learn about the historic inscription that records the achievements and history of the Aksumite kingdom.

The Tombs of King Kaleb and King Gebre Meskel

Discover the royal tombs traditionally associated with these important rulers of ancient Axum.

Yeha Temple

Discover one of the oldest surviving monumental structures in Ethiopia and learn about the ancient civilization of the region.

Dungur – Queen of Sheba's Palace

Explore the archaeological remains of Dungur, traditionally associated with the Queen of Sheba.

Archaeological Museum of Axum

Discover archaeological objects and historical treasures that help explain the remarkable history of the Aksumite Empire.
