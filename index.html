About Your Tour Guide

Tsegay Measho Baraki – Professional Local Tour Guide

My name is Tsegay Measho Baraki. I am from Axum, Ethiopia, one of the most historically important cities in Africa.

I have been working as a local tour guide since 2005 E.C. and hold a legal tour guide license. I am passionate about sharing the history, culture, traditions and heritage of Ethiopia with visitors from around the world.

I offer guided experiences in Axum and other remarkable destinations across Ethiopia. My goal is to help visitors discover the country's historical treasures and cultural diversity.

My Tour Packages

Axum and Yeha Cultural Tour

Tigray Rock Churches Tour

Experience the remarkable rock-hewn churches and spectacular landscapes around Hawzen and other parts of Tigray.

Lalibela – The Rock-Hewn Churches

Discover the magnificent rock-hewn churches of Lalibela, carved directly into solid rock during the reign of King Lalibela. Explore their remarkable architecture, religious history and UNESCO World Heritage significance.

Gondar – The Royal City of Ethiopia

Explore Ethiopia's historic royal capital. Visit Fasil Ghebbi, Fasilides' Castle, Debre Berhan Selassie Church and other remarkable historical monuments. Discover the fascinating stories of Ethiopian emperors and kings.

Bahir Dar – Lake Tana and Blue Nile Falls

Enjoy the beauty of Lake Tana, Ethiopia's largest lake. Visit its historic island monasteries and discover the spectacular Blue Nile Falls. Experience the natural beauty and cultural heritage of the region.

Afar – Dallol, Erta Ale and Afdera

Discover Dallol, famous for its colourful mineral formations, salt plains and unusual geological landscapes.

Experience the extraordinary volcanic scenery of Erta Ale, known for its active lava lake. Explore Afdera and its beautiful salt lake, and learn about the traditional salt-mining activities of the Afar people.

Southern Ethiopia – Culture and Nature

Discover the cultural and natural diversity of Southern Ethiopia. Meet different communities, learn about their unique traditions, traditional lifestyles, colourful cultural celebrations and local crafts. Enjoy beautiful landscapes, mountains, lakes and wildlife.

Harar – The Ancient Walled City

Explore Harar Jugol, a UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for its historic walls, narrow streets, traditional Harari houses, ancient mosques and colourful markets.

Experience the famous traditional hyena feeding ceremony, where local hyena feeders offer food to wild hyenas. Learn about the history and cultural traditions behind this remarkable practice.

Addis Ababa – History and Culture

Discover Ethiopia's historic and vibrant capital city. Visit the National Museum of Ethiopia, home to the famous fossil remains of Lucy, one of the world's important early human discoveries.

Explore Addis Ababa National Park, the Entoto Mountains, Holy Trinity Cathedral, the Ethnological Museum and other important historical and cultural landmarks.
