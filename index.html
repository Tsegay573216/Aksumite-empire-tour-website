Historical and Cultural Tour Destinations

Axum and Tigray – History and Heritage

Discover the remarkable history of the ancient Aksumite Kingdom, one of Africa's great civilizations. From the early centuries CE, Axum developed into an important trading centre connecting Africa, Arabia and the wider Mediterranean world. Its history is reflected in the great stelae, royal tombs, ancient inscriptions and archaeological remains.

Christianity became established in the Aksumite Kingdom during the fourth century. Tigray is also known for its ancient rock-hewn churches, monasteries, religious traditions and distinctive cultural heritage. Discover the region's farming traditions, historic trade, craftsmanship and community life, and understand its important place in the history of Ethiopia.

Afar Region – Dallol, Erta Ale and Afdera

Explore the extraordinary landscapes of the Afar Region and the Danakil Depression. Visit Dallol, famous for its colourful mineral formations and geothermal features, and discover Erta Ale, one of Ethiopia's active volcanoes. Afdera is known for its salt lake and salt-producing landscapes. The region is also home to the Afar people, whose traditions and ways of life are closely connected with this remarkable environment.

Lalibela – The Rock-Hewn Churches

Discover the extraordinary rock-hewn churches of Lalibela, carved directly into volcanic rock during the medieval period, especially under the Zagwe dynasty. Traditionally associated with King Lalibela, these churches became one of Ethiopia's most important centres of Orthodox Christian worship and pilgrimage. Explore their architecture, religious traditions and spiritual significance.

Gondar – The Royal Historic City

Explore Gondar, which became Ethiopia's royal capital under Emperor Fasilides in the seventeenth century. Discover Fasil Ghebbi, the historic royal enclosure with its castles and palaces, as well as Fasilides' Bath and the beautiful church of Debre Berhan Selassie. Gondar offers a remarkable view of Ethiopia's royal history, architecture and cultural traditions.

Bahir Dar – Lake Tana and the Blue Nile Falls

Visit Bahir Dar, located beside Lake Tana, Ethiopia's largest lake and an important source of the Blue Nile. Discover historic island monasteries known for their religious traditions, manuscripts and paintings. Continue to the Blue Nile Falls and enjoy the natural beauty, history and cultural heritage of the region.

Harar – The Historic Walled City

Discover Harar Jugol, the historic walled city known for its important place in Islamic learning, trade and culture in the Horn of Africa. Explore its traditional houses, mosques, shrines, narrow streets and colourful markets. Visitors can also learn about the city's distinctive traditions, including the well-known hyena feeding practice.

Southern Ethiopia – Culture and Natural Heritage

Experience the cultural diversity and natural beauty of southern Ethiopia. Explore the Omo Valley and learn about the traditions and daily lives of its different communities. Discover the distinctive cultural landscapes of Konso and Gedeo, including traditional farming systems, settlements, architecture and local knowledge passed down through generations.

Addis Ababa – The Capital of Ethiopia

Discover Addis Ababa, founded in 1886 under Emperor Menelik II and Empress Taytu. Explore the National Museum of Ethiopia, home to the famous fossil remains of Lucy, and visit the Ethnological Museum, Holy Trinity Cathedral and the historic Entoto area. Learn about Ethiopia's political, cultural and economic history through its capital city.
